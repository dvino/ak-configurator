Their bytes are retained, but this evidence does not establish separate Tap Dance or Key Override semantic equivalence. Typed-codec and firmware-contract limits remain. Only that exact 24-byte span is materialized from the old V2 golden record; all other bytes are retained and the logical values checked by the shared comparison remain equal. A valid persisted V2 record remains byte-identical. Corrupt or partially blank records are rejected.
